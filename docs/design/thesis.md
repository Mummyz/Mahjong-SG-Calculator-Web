# Cuit, the Host — v3 Build Specification

Mahjongyuk is an invitation with a calculator inside it, and the app behaves like a host: it opens on a wall of tiles, hands you objects you can pick up, and tells you who owes what without feeling like a receipt. ONE PHYSICAL LAW replaces the retired grout-and-paper metaphor — every thing you can pick up is a chunky slab with a hard, saturated coloured lip under it, and it sinks 3px when you press it. Exactly four things obey it: the tile, the primary button, the fan medal, the wordmark tile. Nothing else is raised; cards are flat with a hairline, the hand tray is an inset felt well. That closed exception list is enforceable in review. ONE CORRECTNESS LAW, grafted from the runners-up and now the hardest rule here: STATE NEVER LIVES IN THE TILE FACE. Selection, meld-taking and focus live in the lip, the ring and the lift; the face is repainted only when the tile is physically turned over. The shipped tile.css:99-108 repaints .tile__mark to --mj-on-jade on aria-pressed, so today two of the three mandated suit encodings collapse on exactly the tiles the player is staring hardest at — this direction fixes it by construction. Lips are saturated, not tinted: a pale 7px inset reads as an underline, a 4px bar of #A5171D under a near-white face reads as an object. Cuit — a round mint sparrow (麻雀: mahjong is literally "sparrow"; cuit is Indonesian for both a chirp and a nudge) — is the warmth, and he is contained: four appearances, never below 46px, banned from every surface where money is a number. Measured worst text pair anywhere: 4.58:1 (the ×4 stamp on the light end of the tile back); second 4.61:1 (muted on felt, light); third 4.71:1 (萬 on the glossed dark tile face). Every figure here was computed, including the ones the winning pitch skipped.

## Grafts

- STATE NEVER LIVES IN THE TILE FACE (from Night Table, praised by all three of its judges as the idea to steal even if nothing else survived). Selection, meld-taking and focus now live in the lip, the ring and the lift. The winner's own spec repainted rank, mark, rule, glyph and cap to --mj-on-jade on selection — the identical defect the shipped tile.css:99-108 has, where two of the three mandated suit encodings collapse into one flat colour on exactly the tiles the player is staring hardest at. Held = jade lip + 3px jade outer ring + lift. Taken = mandarin lip + DOUBLE ring (outer and inner), so it differs from held by shape as well as hue. The face is untouched in both. The only time the face is repainted is when the tile is physically turned over.
- PIN EVERY COLOUR THAT TOUCHES A TILE FACE TO ITS OWN AUDITED TOKEN (the failure that killed both Mint Condition and Candy Wall — theme-swapping --mj-ink drawn on a face that does not swap, giving 1.09:1 rank numerals and blank honour tiles at night). New tokens --mj-tile-ink, --mj-tile-cap, --mj-bonus-ink, --mj-bonus-cap, --mj-on-back exist for exactly this reason and are measured against the face in both themes: worst case 4.71:1 (萬 under the dark gloss). --mj-ink never appears on a tile.
- THE EXCLAMATION TILE THAT IS ALSO THE 一筒 (from Night Table — judge one said 'whatever direction wins, this comes with it'). The '!' inside the yuk tile is drawn, not typed: a rounded bar over a RING. Read as type it is an exclamation mark; read as a tile face it is the one-dot coin. Both strokes are --mj-on-mandarin at 8.11:1, so the pun costs nothing in contrast, and it makes the 40px compact mark survive without the word.
- THE GRID INVARIANT AS A REVIEW LINT (from Night Table): (360 − 2·pad − (n−1)·gap)/n ≥ 44, applied to every tile grid before drawing. It converts a recurring Design Critic argument into something a reviewer can run. Applied here it kills SubmitWizard's .grid--7, which computes to exactly 40.0px under this direction's 16px padding and 8px gaps; it becomes a horizontal snap rail of 56px tiles, which also stops step 1 adding height to a sheet whose step 2 already overflows 86dvh at 360×780.
- THE THEME-CONSTANT TILE BACK (from Night Table, kept but re-hexed). A face-down tile is a physical object, so --mj-back is the same hex in both themes — but the winner's #0E6B52 is only 2.50:1 against the dark ground, i.e. a dead tile with no silhouette at night. Re-hexed to #12805F → #0C5E46: 3.96:1 on the light mint ground, 3.29:1 on the dark ground, with the ×4 stamp at 4.58:1 on the lightest stop.
- DEPTH SAYS FACE-UP VS IN-HAND (from Night Table). An exposed meld sits on a RAISED --mj-card plinth; a concealed kong sits in a RECESSED --mj-felt well. This replaces the winner's solid --mj-ink panel, which was inherited from the retired look and has no legible dark-theme equivalent, and it lets app.css delete the `filter: saturate(.6) brightness(.96)` hack on exposed meld tiles.
- THE LEDGER SEAT GLYPH PROMOTED TO A REAL TILE (from Night Table, and it is the answer to judge one's 'my payout screen is the plainest surface in the whole app'). The flat .seatchip becomes a 34×42 wind tile drawn with the full recipe — face, saturated lip, shadow. The app's own material reaches the money screen without a cartoon standing next to a number.
- THE PIP STRIP AS THE SUIT'S OWN ILLUSTRATION (from Candy Wall, which all three of its judges told us to steal regardless of who won). One solid bar for 萬 (a brush stroke), three dots for 筒 (coins), three dashes for 索 (sticks). Confirmed and kept, drawn as background-image on the .tile__rule element that already exists — the current border-bottom construction has height:0 and physically cannot draw dots at 3px. Cuit's tail is the same three dashes.
- MONEY DIRECTION IS NEVER COLOUR-ONLY, AND THE SIGN IS COMPUTED FROM THE READER'S SEAT (sign convention from Night Table and Mint Condition; the reader-relative rule is this spec's hardening of judge one's complaint about 'a column of red minus signs on the night I won'). Money arriving at you: '+' in --mj-collect. Money leaving you: '−' in --mj-pay. A transfer between two other players: neutral --mj-ink with '→'. The verb is always spelled out. The current Results.tsx renders every other player's row as formatMoney(-units) with class `pay`, so a winning night paints red.
- THE DOCK IS A GRADIENT FADE INTO THE GROUND, NOT A BLUR (from Mint Condition). Content dissolves under the actions instead of hitting a border. This also removes the winner's backdrop-filter, which was pure cost: the dock is a flex sibling of .scroll, not an overlay, so there was nothing behind it to blur.
- BASE-EDGE COLOUR AS A FAMILY TAXONOMY (from Mint Condition). The lip tells you what class of object you are holding before you read anything: suit lips per suit, slate for winds, gold for bonus, pine for a face-down tile. But the lips are SATURATED, not tinted — Mint Condition's own owner-judge killed the pale version outright ('7px of pale mint reads as a soft underline, not thickness'), so every lip here is a full-strength colour clearing 3:1 against both its own face and the page ground.
- THE FUNCTIONAL GRADIENT DIRECTION (from Mint Condition). The page runs peach-cream at the top where you read to mint at the bottom where your thumb is, so the mandarin primary button always lands on mint at maximum hue separation from its own fill. The gradient earns its place by argument rather than mood.

## Dropped

- CUIT AT 28PX IN THE PREDICTION PANEL HEADER — killed outright. Rendered rather than described he is an unreadable mint smudge, and that header is the mascot's highest-frequency appearance in the app. That header now carries NO bird at all. Cuit's floor is 46px everywhere, and he appears in exactly four places: the landing lockup (46px), the tile-set screen (56px), the prediction panel's not-enough-tiles BODY (56px), and beside the fan medal (64px). He remains banned from every surface where money is a number.
- THE ROTATED, OVERLAPPING THREE-TILE FAN ON THE GAME CARDS — killed. At −8°/0°/+8° with −10px overlap the middle tile is half-buried and the animal tile loses its caption, so the art meant to teach 'animals vs none' teaches nothing. The cards now show three real 52px tiles side by side, no rotation, no overlap, every caption legible.
- THE 300PX PEACH RADIAL BLOB ON THE LANDING — killed twice over. It is the single most template-shaped element in the direction (judge three named the backdrop as the tell), and positioned at top:-70px right:-80px inside a .scroll whose overflow-y:auto computes overflow-x to auto, it created horizontal scroll on the front door. Replaced by the hero wall: a clipped row of real tile backs peeking down from the top edge, product-derived and impossible to mistake for a wellness app.
- BACKDROP-FILTER ON THE DOCK — killed. The dock is a flex sibling of .scroll, not an overlay; it blurs a static gradient and buys nothing but a compositing layer. Replaced by a gradient fade into --mj-ground-2, which also removes the @supports fallback branch.
- THE LIGHT-THEME RULE THAT CARDS CARRY SHADOWS AND NO BORDERS — killed. White card on the mint ground measures 1.24:1 and the empty tray slot measured 1.10:1; with the grout gone, a 6%-alpha shadow is not structure on a phone at low brightness. The hairline now ships in BOTH themes at 2.17:1 on card, and the empty slot gets a solid 2px --mj-slot-edge ring at 3.55:1 on felt — a real 3:1 boundary, because that slot is how a player sees how many tiles they still owe.
- THE GHOST TILE DRAWN WITH OPACITY — killed. Both runner-up panels caught it independently: 55% opacity drags the coloured CJK mark and the pip strip below AA together, so two of the three mandated encodings die on the one screen whose whole job is naming tiles you do not have. The ghost is now full-strength ink with NO lip and NO drop shadow — absence of weight, not absence of ink — plus a dashed --mj-slot-edge ring and a '+' pip.
- THE GHOST TILE SHIPPING WITH NO ACCESSIBLE NAME — killed. Tile.tsx already routes every other state through a t() label (tile.notNowName, tile.exhaustedName, tile.heldName); solid-vs-ghost was declared 'the entire visual grammar of prediction' and specified purely as an outline. A screen reader would hear the same name for 'you have it' and 'you still need it'. New `needed` prop, new key tile.neededName.
- --mj-disabled #849692 ON THE DARK DISABLED FILL — killed. It measures 4.33:1, not the '4.58:1 worst case in both themes' the winning pitch claimed; it is also below the bar this repo already holds itself to (tokens.css:31, 'inactive, but still legible'). Now #93A6A1 at 5.28:1.
- --mj-gloss AT .10 ALPHA IN DARK — killed. It lifts the dark tile face to #43534E, dropping 萬 to 3.74:1 and 筒 to 4.23:1 at 11px, on the tray, the one screen where a player audits their own hand. Now .06, lifting the face only to #36443E, worst suit 4.71:1. And the gloss layer is removed entirely from mini tiles — below 48px it only costs contrast and paint.
- THE BODY GRADIENT — killed as written. .shell paints an opaque var(--mj-ground) across the whole viewport, so a gradient on body would never have rendered and the direction's signature background would have been invisible on day one. The gradient is painted on .shell; body keeps a flat --mj-ground-2 for the area beside the column.
- THE 'DECORATIVE INSET FRAME ::after' ON THE FACE-DOWN TILE — killed. ::after is already double-booked between the 白 frame (tile.css:69) and the exhausted label (tile.css:154). The face-down frame moves to ::before, since the gloss is meaningless on a tile lying face down, and the exhausted block is required to sit AFTER the [data-h="P"] block, because the two ::after rules have identical specificity and source order decides.
- gap: 8px ON TILE GRIDS — killed. A 4px lip was unaccounted for in four grids and in .tray__group's 2px padding, so lips would collide with the tile below. Row gap is now --gap-row: 12px = 8 + the lip, columns stay at 8px so the 5-column width math (59.2px) is unchanged. The count badge gets z-index:2 now that .tile is overflow:visible.
- .grid--7 IN SubmitWizard — killed. Under this direction's 16px padding and 8px gaps it computes to exactly 40.0px, below the 44px floor. Replaced by a horizontal snap rail of 56px tiles, which also avoids adding a third row of height to a sheet whose own code comment says step 2 already overflows 86dvh at 360×780.
- CUIT'S SUPPLIED SVG GEOMETRY — killed and redrawn. The beak path M32 30 l9 5 -9 5 overlapped the right eye at cx=39 cy=28 r=3.2, and the tail described as 'three fanned strokes' was a single closed subpath. The beak is now a small diamond between the eyes with 1.7px of clearance, and the tail is three actual stroked paths — which is what makes it rhyme with the 索 rule.
- THE CLAIM 'THE WORST TEXT/BACKGROUND RATIO ANYWHERE IS 4.58:1 IN BOTH THEMES' — killed as a claim and replaced by a measurement. The real worst pairs in this system are 4.58:1 (×4 stamp on the light end of the tile back), 4.61:1 (muted on light felt), 4.68:1 (disabled on its fill, light), 4.71:1 (萬 on the glossed dark face) and 4.91:1 (muted on the light mint ground). No text pair anywhere falls below 4.58:1.
- THE ALL-RED LEDGER — killed. See grafts: sign and colour are now computed from the reader's seat, so a night you won reads green.
- UPPERCASE, LETTER-SPACED LABELS — killed everywhere, as the retiring look's strongest document tell, and because Bahasa Indonesia runs 20–30% longer: a tracked-out uppercase label does not fit a 360px button. --f-sign, --f-num and every text-transform: uppercase in app.css and tile.css go with it. The only surviving all-caps text is the two-to-five-letter Latin cap on an honour tile, which is a proper noun.
