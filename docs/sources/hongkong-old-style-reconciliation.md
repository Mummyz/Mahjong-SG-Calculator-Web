# RECONCILIATION — Hong Kong Old Style (香港舊章／清章) Mahjong Scoring

All quotes below are reproduced from the four gatherer digests. I did not re-fetch the pages; every number I state is attached to a quote one or more gatherers reported verbatim, with its URL. Where I say a value is "better supported," that is a judgement about source independence and authority, and I say so explicitly. Where a value is a coin-flip between real conventions, I say that too, and do not pick for you silently.

---

## 0. SOURCE INDEPENDENCE — read this before weighing any "3 sources agree"

The thirteen-odd URLs across the four digests collapse into **five independent Old-Style families**. Counting URLs instead of families would triple-count.

**FAMILY A — Chinese-language Wikipedia.** `zh-yue.wikipedia.org/wiki/香港蔴雀食糊牌型`, `zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/香港麻雀胡牌列表`, `zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hk/香港麻雀常用番種`, `zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/香港麻雀`. Gatherer 3: "THE zh AND zh-yue WIKIPEDIA ARTICLES ARE NOT INDEPENDENT OF EACH OTHER. Their fan tables and their 分數計算（全銃制) money tables are identical." Self-identifies as Old Style — zh-yue opens 「呢篇文列香港麻雀（另稱「清章」或「舊章」）嘅食糊牌型同埋準則。」 and zh 常用番種 says 「實際上是屬於較傳統的『清章』（一說『舊章』）打法，有別於新章的廣東麻雀。」 **Weakness (Gatherer 1):** zh-yue 食糊牌型 carries `{{unreferenced}}` in its wikitext. Treat as community consensus, not a documented rule.

**FAMILY B — mahjong.wikidot.com HKOS pages.** Plus two dependents: the bpmahjong PDF, whose own header reads "Source page: Hong Kong Old Style Scoring at http://mahjong.wikidot.com/rules:hong-kong-old-style-scoring", footer "page revision: 44, last edited: 4 Apr 2013" — i.e. an *older revision of the same page*, not a second authority (Gatherer 3 is right to flag this); and Mahjong 4 Friends, whose special-hand values (All Honors 10, Blessing of Earth 10, Blessing of Heaven 10, Small Winds 10, Thirteen Orphans 13, Big Winds 15, Nine Gates 15, Eighteen Arhats 18) are **identical to wikidot's, hand for hand**. I cannot prove derivation, but I will not count M4F as independent corroboration of family B's outlier numbers.

**FAMILY C — 香港麻雀協會 / GGHK official tournament rulebook**, `GGHK-Classical-Hong-Kong-Mahjong-Rulebook-CN.pdf`, rev. 2023-05-17. The only *institutional* Old-Style source in the set. HKMA's own index page equates the names: 「香港麻雀 Hong Kong Mahjong 又名 清章麻雀、廣東牌(僅限香港本地稱呼)、Classical Mahjong」.

**FAMILY D — Alan Kwan Shiu Ho, 中庸麻雀史觀 第七章 ～ 簡化：香港「舊章」麻雀** (2009), HTML title literally "Hong Kong Old Style mahjong". A named author writing a *derivation* of the ruleset. No full fan table, but decisive on payment and the dealer.

**FAMILY E — English Wikipedia's `Mahjong` article, sections "Old Hong Kong mahjong rules" / "Old Hong Kong scoring" / "Base points (Old Hong Kong Simplified)".** Old-Style-labelled, but note: it is a **base-point** system (3 faan → 1 base point, 10+ → "8 (limit)"), structurally unlike every faan-to-money table in families A/B/C. It is the *only* Old-Style-labelled source asserting dealer doubling. See §6.

**NOT Old Style — contrast only.** `en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_mahjong_scoring_rules` self-identifies: "In typical Hong Kong mahjong (also known as Hong Kong New Style)…" and punts: "There's also documentation of more archaic scoring systems, commonly referred to as Hong Kong Old Style. These can be viewed here." Gatherer 1 further established that the capital-M title given in the original task is a bare redirect — raw content exactly `#REDIRECT [[Hong Kong mahjong scoring rules]]`. **The article named in the task brief describes New Style and says almost nothing about Old Style.** Also non-Old-Style: PlayEA HK, Cosmopolitan HK, 友和 YOHO, mahjonggame.hk, TileBuddy, CoolOldGames, TheRuleBook, mahjongscoring.org. McGill Mahjong is Old-Style-labelled but gives no per-hand faan numbers.

**Nothing here is Singapore, Riichi, Taiwanese 16-tile, MCR, or American.** All four gatherers independently report zero mentions of animal tiles anywhere. That is an absence, not an assertion; only English Wikipedia positively places animals elsewhere: "In Singapore and Malaysia an extra set of bonus tiles of four animals are used."

## 1. TILE SET — 144, flowers and seasons included, no animals

**The 108 + 16 + 12 core is unanimous. The only disagreement is whether the 8 bonus tiles are standard equipment or an option.**

**144 stated flat:**
- Mahjong 4 Friends (Family B, HKOS-labelled): "Hong Kong Old Style Mahjong is played with a standard Mahjong tile set, consisting of 144 tiles" — with the exact breakdown asked for: "each suit contains four identical tiles (36 tiles per suit for 108 total)" / "four of each Wind tile (16 total) and four of each Dragon tile (12 total)" / "Four Flower tiles and four Season tiles make up the eight bonus tiles." → 108+16+12+4+4 = 144.
- English Wikipedia, Old Hong Kong section (Family E): "Old Hong Kong mahjong is played with a standard set of 144 mahjong tiles". Its table gives Dots 36, Bamboo 36, Characters 36, Winds 16, Dragons 12, Flowers 4, Seasons 4, Total 144. And on bonus tile uniqueness: "there is only one of each bonus tile, so there are a total of four flower and four season tiles in the set."
- 香港麻雀 / 香港蔴雀 (Family A): 「標準嘅香港蔴雀會使用上述144隻牌。如果唔打花牌，噉洗牌前就抽起兩臺花先，淨係用136隻牌。」 Composition: 「一至九筒、一至九索、一至九萬，以及東、南、西、北、中、發、白呢七隻番子，上述每款牌有四隻。另外仲要兩臺花，通常爲：春、夏、秋、冬；梅、蘭、菊、竹。」 Wall confirms both ways: 「每人將其中36隻牌上下排成18棟（如果唔打花牌，則每人將其中34隻牌上下排成17棟）」.

**136 + optional flowers:**
- mahjong.wikidot Overview (Family B): infobox "Tiles | 136, 144"; body "A basic set of 136 tiles. Playing with 8 Flower tiles is common." / "Dice : 2 Dice if playing without Flowers. 3 Dice if playing with Flowers."
- HKMA/GGHK (Family C): 「採用一副136張的麻雀牌,視乎規則選用8張花牌。」 with 27 suited types × 4 = 108 and 7 honour types × 4 = 28. **This official rulebook never writes the number 144.**

**VERDICT.** 144 with flowers and seasons is correct for Hong Kong Old Style *as normally played*, and Family A states it explicitly as 標準. But note plainly: **no Old-Style source states 144 as mandatory** (Gatherer 4's finding, and it holds). Playing without flowers is a real, named option (「如果唔打花牌」 / "if the group is playing with Flowers"), and it changes the wall length, the dice count, and — per Family A — whether 門前清 scores at all (§4). Building an app: 144 is the right default; flowers-off should be a toggle, not an impossibility.

Flower/season → seat mapping, stated identically in two families: 「春、梅代表東位；夏、蘭代表南位；秋、菊代表西位；冬、竹代表北位」 (Family A) and 「花牌上的號碼1等於東，2等於南，3等於西，4等於北」 (Family A, 常用番種).

## 2. MINIMUM FAN — 3, and every source that discusses it calls it configurable

**Every family that names a minimum names 3.**

- Family C (official, and here **not** optional): 「三番起糊：手牌必須有三番以上才可以食糊。」 and in the 食糊 chapter 「三番起糊：手牌別須要有3番或以上，才可以食糊。」
- Family A: 「不過現時一般唔承認雞糊；打較大上限番數嗰陣，食糊最少要有三番，叫做「三番起糊」。」 and, stating the configurability in its own words, 「根據自訂規則，現時通常以三番起糊」.
- Family B: "Decide on a minimum number of fan needed before calling mahjong (3 fan is common)." (2013 revision) / "1970s: The 3-3 System is adopted. Hands must be 3 Fan to go out and the value of All Pungs and Half Flush are increased to 3 Fan." / M4F: "On Mahjong 4 Friends, the minimum requirement is 3 fan."
- Family E: "The winner must have a legal hand that meets the minimum ''faan'' points agreed to in advance" / "at least the minimum ''faan'' value agreed in advance (often 3)".

**Family A gives the full historical ladder** — this is the strongest evidence that the minimum is a dial, not a rule: 「香港蔴雀本身係可以無番食糊，叫做「雞糊」，以前多數打「雞四」，亦即係雞糊「零番」嚟做起糊，四番就封頂。」 then 「初時為一番起糊…後來又發覺一番起糊「未夠喉」…所以逐漸演變成兩番、甚至三番起糊嘅玩法。」

**Family D dissents on its status, not its value.** Alan Kwan: 「開始時雞和不准和，漸漸地越加越高，到近年以三番起和為主流。」 and 「「三番起和」及所謂「全銃」流行了起來，又受到「集體失憶」的侵蝕，今日不少雀迷都以為那些是舊章的正宗當然規則，不知道其實是近年的演變。」 He adds 「筆者是不支持起和限制的」. So: 3-faan is mainstream *and recent*; a source that actually studied the history says it is not "the" old-style rule.

**GENUINE CONFLICTS, both against 3:**
1. **mahjong.wikidot Overview infobox: "Minimum | 0-2, 3 with Flowers".** This is Family B's own *baseline* HKOS minimum, and it can be zero. It contradicts every other source's headline number and even its own scoring page's "3 fan is common". Gatherer 4 flagged it correctly. Note the internal logic: with flowers in play, cheap fan is easy, so the floor rises.
2. **CoolOldGames (not Old Style):** "focus on achieving the base requirement of 1 fan in your winning hand… it is perfectly acceptable to win a hand worth 1 fan." Reported for completeness; not an Old-Style source and outvoted.

**VERDICT: 3 faan, exposed as a setting (0/1/2/3 all attested as real historical settings).** The 3 is a convention, extremely well attested as the modern default, and one good source says explicitly it is a 20th-century development.

Sub-minimum win = 詐糊 (false win), not a scoreless win: 「食糊不夠起糊番數（通常係三番）」 (Family A). Penalty in §7.

## 3. THE LIMIT — no single number exists, and the task's vocabulary premise is wrong

### 3a. Vocabulary correction — 辣 is NOT the cap

The brief asks for "the LIMIT (laak / 辣 / 爆)". **Three of four gatherers independently found that 辣 in these sources names the shape of the doubling curve, not the ceiling.** This matters because an app that labels its cap setting 辣 will be labelling the wrong control.

- Family A: 「傳統蔴雀以四番為滿糊，隨後以半辣上，每兩番先至跳一倍。（賭得比較大嘅人亦會辣辣上，每一番跳一倍。）」 — 半辣 = double every **two** fan; 辣辣 = double every **one** fan.
- Family C uses it the same way in its table header: 「分數按100底半辣上計算」.
- Family B names its table "The Fan Table (Half-Spicy)"; English Wikipedia: "two common ways to translate faan: 'full spicy' and 'half spicy'."

**The cap has its own words:**
- **例牌 / 上限番數** — the limit hands and the ceiling (Families A and C).
- **爆棚 / 細爆** — Family A, and this is the *only* family that uses 爆 for the cap: 「上限番數嘅食糊牌型就爲「例牌」，一般會採用八番例牌、十番例牌或者十三番例牌，叫做「爆棚」（相當於日本蔴雀嘅役滿）。」 Its money table labels the rungs 「4（滿糊）」、「10（細爆）」、「13（爆棚）」. Gatherers 2 and 4 reported finding no 爆-for-cap usage; Gatherers 1 and 3 found it in zh-yue. **Resolution: 爆棚 for the cap is attested, in one family only.**
- **勒 (laak)** — Family D, and this is where the English "laak" actually comes from: 「「勒」的用字，相信是取其「限制」的意思」, in a 複式滿貫制: 「例如定五番為一勒（收32籌），第六番不加分，有七番便算雙勒（收64籌），九番算三勒（收128籌），為最大的收入，滿貫和種當三勒計。」 Family B's 2013 revision uses the English term for the same object: "The table below is called a fan-laak table. In this one, certain fan values convert to the same amount of points. This helps to limit the scores."
- **雙辣／三辣** — a *third* thing again, pinned to the old 4-faan 滿糊: 「「雙辣」同「三辣」即係按四番嘅價錢，「雙辣」以倍半或者雙倍計算、同「三辣」以雙倍或者三倍計算…而雙辣同三辣就控制最高注碼喺四番嘅兩倍…同四番嘅三倍」 (Family A).

### 3b. The cap value — 8, 10, or 13, all real

| Cap | Source | Quote |
|---|---|---|
| **10, hard, all limit hands flattened to it** | Family C (official) | 「例牌及上限番數：以10番為上限，例牌一律算10番。」 |
| **10, common but table-set** | Family B | "Most players set a maximum Fan limit. 10 is common." / "In the case of a 10 Fan limit, a hand worth 10 Fan or more would be worth 128 Points." Overview infobox: "Maximum | Varies" |
| **8 or 10; 13 online** | Family A | 「根據自訂規則，現時通常以三番起糊，最大以打八番或十番為上限（網絡遊戲比較常見最大打十三番）。」 |
| **8** | M4F (Family B dependent) | "the fan limit, the maximum scoring cap, is 8" — its conversion table stops at 8 fan = 64 points |
| **13+** | McGill (HKOS-labelled) | "If you score a limit hand, do not count faan at all, simply score it as a 13+ faan win (The limit of how much your hand can score)." |
| **8 (base points), at 10+ faan** | Family E | "Base points (Old Hong Kong Simplified)" row: "10+ \|\| 8 (limit)" |
| **None** | Family A | 「賭得比較大嘅人會選擇打無限番，呢個時候例牌亦都按普通番種計算番數（例如大四喜複計混一色對對糊或者字一色，加起嚟就超過十三番例牌）；所以「天糊」同「地糊」等奇牌，一定要事先說明例牌番數。」 |

**Payout the cap corresponds to** (all from §5's tables):
- Family A 全銃制: 10 (細爆) → 出銃 128, 自摸 192 (64×3). 13 (爆棚) → 出銃 384, 自摸 576 (192×3).
- Family C at 100底: 10番 → 出銃 512, 自摸 256×3.
- Family B: 10-faan limit → 128 Points, then ×2/×1 or ×2×2×2 per the payout rule.
- Family B 2013 fan-laak variant: 10+ → 64 points.
- McGill: 13+ → 256 off the discarder, or 128 from each on self-pick.
- Family E: 10+ → 8 base points; separately "A common scoring limit is 64 points, which is the highest base points doubled twice. A winner receives the scoring limit from each player without any doubling."

**VERDICT: there is no fact here, only a setting.** 10 is the best-supported default: it is what the only official body specifies (「以10番為上限」), what Family B calls common, and one of the two Family A names it. **8 and 13 are equally legitimate and equally attested.** Ship it as a setting with 10 default, 8 and 13 offered, and — if you want to be faithful to Family A — an unlimited option.

**One conflict inside a single source, unresolved:** Family B's half-spicy ladder prints 11→**196**, 12→**264**, whereas Family A and English Wikipedia both give 11→192, 12→256. All three gatherers who saw it flagged 196 as breaking the page's own ×1.5/×2 pattern. **Do not silently correct it, and do not adopt it** — 192/256 is supported by two families against one page's unexplained numbers.

### 4b. Fully Concealed 門前清 — a real structural disagreement, not just a number

- **C scores it at 1:** 「門前清 食糊時所有手牌皆為暗手，沒有明章。（暗槓屬於明章）」
- **A scores it at 1** — 「門前清（門清）：沒有上、碰、槓（包括暗槓）任何牌而食糊。」 — **but adds a killer caveat:** 「如果有打花牌就不計門前清這個番種。」 (if flowers are in play, 門前清 is not counted).
- **B does not have it at all in its HKOS "Clear Chapter" list.** Two gatherers independently verified this. "Concealed Hand | 1" and "Fully Concealed | 3" appear only in B's *New Chapter* column.
- E has it at 1 only in its New Style article.

But note that A's flower caveat and B's omission point the same direction: **in flower-play HKOS, the concealed-hand fan is frequently not scored.** This is a design decision you must make explicitly, not a number you can look up. Note also the exact definition differs: A excludes concealed kongs (「包括暗槓」), C says 「暗槓屬於明章」 — same result, opposite phrasing.

## 5. PAYMENT MODEL — this is the part the brief most needs, and the answer is cleaner than it looks

### 5a. Both conventions are real, both are named in Cantonese, and the sources name them identically

| | Cantonese name | Discard win | Self-draw win |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HALF PAYMENT** | 陪銃制 / 半銃制 / 出銃么二制 | discarder pays **2 units**, each other loser pays **1 unit** → total **4** | each of three pays **2 units** → total **6** |
| **FULL PAYMENT** | 全銃制 | discarder pays **4 units**, others pay **nothing** → total **4** | each of three pays **2 units** → total **6** |

**The critical structural finding, which three of four gatherers reached independently: the two conventions differ ONLY in how the discard loss is distributed. The totals are identical, and the self-draw rule is identical. In both, a self-draw pays the winner exactly 1.5× a discard win, split three ways.**

This is stated most explicitly by Family B, which uses the identical Points table for both branches and changes only the split:
- Clear Chapter (= HKOS proper): "Self-Draw: Each player pays the winner Points x2." / "Discard: The discarder pays the winner Points x2, the other two opponents pay Points x1." Overview infobox: "Self-Draw Split | x2 x2 x2", "Discard Split | x2 x1 x1".
- New Chapter: "Self-Draw: Each player pays the winner Points x2." / "Discard: The discarder pays the winner Points x4, the other two opponents pay nothing." Framing: "'New Chapter' rulesets use the simplified scoring system of 'Clear Chapter', but with a Discarder-Pays-All payout system."

And Family D explains *why* the 1.5× survives into 全銃: 「但是這其實不是真正的全銃，因為有「出銃么二制」自摸 1.5 倍的副作用加上舊章自摸的一番，實際上自摸有三倍的收入」.

### 5b. Which convention is Old Style? Family D answers this directly, and it is the best answer available

**Alan Kwan (Family D) states that half-payment IS the defining change that created 舊章:**
> 「其實從古典麻雀演變到舊章麻雀，只需要從兩點出發：廢除副點（光算番數），及把授受法轉為「出銃么二制」。」
> 「針對這問題，漸漸有人採用了「出銃么二制」：要付雙倍的不是莊家，而改為由放銃者取代。」
> 「但是自摸的情形怎樣算呢？因為「出銃么二制」是以放銃者代替「莊家么二制」裏的莊家，所以自然地，自摸時便以和牌者代替莊家，也就是說，和牌者向三家各收取雙倍的分數。」

And that full payment is a modern import:
> 「舊章本來是採用「出銃么二制」的，但近年受了日本麻雀及台灣麻雀等影響，也流行起所謂「全銃」」

**Family A corroborates that half-payment is the traditional one:** 「傳統廣東麻雀是陪銃制，即如果甲出銃給乙，使玩家乙食糊，甲要支付本身出銃的金額，而丙和丁則須支付甲的金額的一半。」 (Note this is the same 2:1:1 ratio expressed the other way round.) A also defines the other: 「全銃制就是出銃者要同時替其他玩家支付應付款額，即甲要支付自己、丙和丁的款項給乙。」

**But — and this is decisive for present-day practice — every source that publishes an actual money table publishes it for 全銃:**
- Family A's only table is headed 「分數計算（全銃制）」.
- Family C, the official body, offers **only** full payment: 「得分授受法 1.全銃：食出銃時由出銃者包付所有分數。」

**Family B assigns half-payment to HKOS proper and full payment to New Chapter, which is exactly the opposite emphasis from what A and C's tables show.**

**VERDICT.** Both are Hong Kong Old Style. **Half payment (2:1:1) is historically the original** — this rests on Family D, a named author writing a derivation, plus Family A's 「傳統…是陪銃制」, and is the strongest historical claim in the whole corpus. **Full payment (全銃) is what the modern tables and the official rulebook use.** This is a setting, and a source explicitly tells you to agree it in advance: "Make sure your mahjong group agrees on a method of payment before starting a game." Ship both; either default is defensible. **The self-draw side needs no setting at all — it is the same under both.**

Its own table at 3 faan says 出銃 **8**, 自摸 4×3. 16 ≠ 8, and a 16 discard against a 12 self-draw total would invert the 1.5× ratio the whole table is built on. **I cannot reconcile this and I am not guessing which figure is the error. Use the table, which is internally consistent across fourteen rows.**

### 5e. The one source that contradicts everyone — Cosmopolitan HK (NOT Old Style)

Its prose says half payment — 「出銃者則需要付雙份錢，其餘兩家則只需付單份」 (2:1:1) — but its worked example says 4:1:1: 「三番糊自摸，則位位$8；若是出銃，則是出銃者付$16、其餘兩家則付每位$4」. That makes discard total $24 = self-draw total $24, breaking the 1.5× ratio every other source keeps. **Self-contradictory, unlabelled as to convention, and not an Old-Style source. Discard it.** (CoolOldGames is similarly unusable: it never says what the non-discarders pay.)

**Non-Old-Style corroboration of the naming**, for what it is worth — English Wikipedia: "In discarder-pays-all, the discarder pays the winner the full points. In discarder-pays-half, only half the awarded points are paid the discarder; the other half is split evenly between and paid by the two remaining players." / "When a player self-draws their winning tile, all players pay. The points awarded are 1.5 times the table amount, which also ensures the winner's total is divisible by three." And mahjongscoring.org: "The standard self draw bonus is for the winner to receive double the base points from every player, which in the Fan to Points Table above comes out to 1.5x the points value." Both confirm the 1.5× structure independently of the Old-Style families.

## 6. THE DEALER

### 6a. Does the dealer pay or receive double? NO — and this is a positive finding, not a silence

**Family D explains that the doubling was deliberately moved OFF the dealer and ONTO the discarder. This is the single most valuable quote in the entire corpus for this question:**
> 「古典麻雀原本採用「莊家么二制」的授受法…一般的和牌都是按「閒一莊二」的比率攤付，不會追究放銃責任。」
> 「特別是閒家放銃時，放銃者只輸一倍，反而莊家卻要輸雙倍，自然會覺得不平。」
> 「針對這問題，漸漸有人採用了「出銃么二制」：要付雙倍的不是莊家，而改為由放銃者取代。」

**Family B says it flatly:** Overview infobox "Dealer Bonus | None" / "Dealer Curse | None" / "Payout | Winner only". Its payout rules contain no dealer term.

**Family C, the official rulebook, has no dealer clause in its 得分授受法 chapter at all** — the only payment modifiers it names are 全銃 and 包牌. The dealer appears only in seating, rotation, and procedure.

**Family A's 全銃制 table has three columns — 番數 / 出銃 / 自摸 — and no dealer row, column, or note.** Gatherer 1 adds an important methodological point: a dealer-multiplier sentence exists in the zh-yue page's *wikitext* but is inside an HTML comment, so it is not article text and was correctly not quoted as content.

**THE ONE OLD-STYLE-LABELLED SOURCE THAT DISAGREES — Family E, English Wikipedia's "Old Hong Kong scoring":**
> "* If the winner is east, all losers double the base points.
> * If the east player is a losing player, they pay double the points to the winner."
> "Hong Kong mahjong is essentially a payment system of doubling and redoubling, where winning from the wall adds great value to the final payment and where the dealer is highly rewarded or penalised if they win or lose."

with a worked example: "East wins with 10 ''faan'' (8 base points) on discard from west (double penalty to West) | East (dealer) || '''16''' (from south) + '''32''' (from west) + '''16''' (from north) = '''+64'''".

**ASSESSMENT — this one is not a coin flip.** Family E's dealer doubling is embedded in a base-point-and-double system that no other Old-Style source uses, and Family D specifically identifies dealer doubling (「莊家么二制」/「閒一莊二」) as the **Chinese Classical** predecessor that Old Style *replaced*. Family E is, on this point, describing the ancestor. Against it stand: an affirmative denial (B), a structural explanation of the removal (D), and two payment tables with no dealer term (A, C). **Verdict: no dealer multiplier in Hong Kong Old Style. Do not implement one.**

Two further claims, both from non-Old-Style sources, reported for completeness and rejected on the same grounds: TheRuleBook — "The dealer (East seat) pays and collects double…" (equates HK mahjong with 廣東麻雀, uncorroborated by any Cantonese or HKOS source); mahjongscoring.org, which at least labels it correctly as optional — "Some variants will modify scores if the winner or discarder is the dealer" / "Dealer Wins Double" / "Dealer Loses Double".

### 6b. When the dealer wins — UNANIMOUS: the dealer repeats

- Family A: 「如果冇任何玩家贏出（即係流局），或者莊家自己贏咗，就要「冧莊」…佢會繼續擔任莊家…各人嘅門風亦都唔會變」; 「如果嗰局由閒家贏咗…就會由本來坐喺南位嘅玩家擔任莊家，稱爲「過莊」」.
- Family B: "If East wins the Hand, the Seat Winds do not rotate. East gets to be Dealer again." / "If Non-East wins the Hand or if it's a Draw, the Seat Winds rotate counter-clockwise."
- Family C: 「輪莊：打完一局後，東家和了（食糊）…會繼續擔任莊家；否則輪到之前的南家做下一局的莊家。一圈裡四家各有一次做莊機會。」
- Family E: "If the dealer wins the hand, they will remain the dealer, and an extra hand is played in addition to the minimum 16 hands in a match."
- Also: 「莊家即每局嘅東家，而其餘三家就稱爲閒家。」 (A) and 「東家又稱為「莊家」，其餘三家統稱為「閒家」。」 (C).

### 6c. When the hand is a washout — GENUINELY SPLIT, no resolution available

| Washout → | Source | Quote |
|---|---|---|
| **Dealer KEEPS the deal** | Family A | 「如果冇任何玩家贏出（即係流局）…就要「冧莊」」 |
| | Family E | "if there is no winner (a draw or \"goulash hand\"), an extra hand is played with the same seating positions and prevailing wind" |
| | McGill (implied) | its Eight Dealer Keeps rule counts draws as non-shifting hands |
| | TheRuleBook (not HKOS) | "if the round ends in a draw, the dealer position stays" |
| **Deal PASSES** | Family B | "If Non-East wins the Hand **or if it's a Draw**, the Seat Winds rotate counter-clockwise." |
| | Family C | 「流局時莊家不會連莊，即使是北四局亦如是。」 |
| | Family A, as a common shortcut | 「爲咗加快雀局嘅進行，亦有唔少玩家喺流局嗰陣會照樣過莊，淨係得莊家自己贏咗先要冧莊。」 |

**Family C contradicts itself**, and two gatherers caught it independently: its 輪莊 clause says the dealer keeps the deal 「在部分流局的情況下」 while its 流局 clause says flatly 「流局時莊家不會連莊」. Neither gatherer would choose between them and neither will I; the 流局 clause is the one written specifically for this situation.

**VERDICT: convention, not fact.** Both behaviours are attested within single sources (Family A gives the traditional rule *and* the common shortcut in consecutive sentences). Make it a setting. Family E notes the practical reason: "This means that a match may potentially have no limit to the number of hands played (though some players will set a limit of three consecutive hands…)". Family B hedges the same way: "Continuous Dealer Wins | Varies".

### 6d. Match structure — agreed

Family B: "A Round of Mahjong is made up of 4 Hands. There are 4 Rounds in a Game." Family C: 「當輪莊已執行四次，起莊再次擔任莊家時，該輪比賽則被視爲進行了一圈。」 Family E implies the same with "the minimum 16 hands in a match". Family C also: 「場風（圈風）和自風（門風）可疊加計算。」

## 7. PENALTIES AND LIABILITY (包牌 / 詐糊) — bonus, since the gatherers found solid quotes

**包牌 (pao / liability): one player absorbs the entire loss.** The rule differs by payment convention, and Family A states both:
- Under 陪銃制: 「根據傳統，如果打陪銃制：任何食糊嘅九章落地包出銃（包一半）、十二章落地包自摸。」
- Under 全銃制: 「以下係打全銃制，通常嘅包牌罰則包括：清一色落地：十二章落地…要包自摸。…大三元落地：中發白落地包自摸…大四喜落地：東南西北落地包自摸…十八羅漢落地：四槓落地包自摸」
- Family C names the same devices: 「十二章包（張張包）」, 「大三元包」, plus 「搶槓當自摸：搶槓視為開槓者出銃，加計一番，但支付分數時按「自摸」計算」.
- Family A also: 「槓上開花…明槓出槓者包。」

**詐糊 (false win): pays the maximum, to each player, as a self-draw.**
- Family A: 「因為食詐糊者阻礙咗牌局嘅進行…所以食咗詐糊必須對每家賠出番數上限嘅分數。即係說如果打最大八番，就要每家賠自摸八番價錢。」 and 「食詐糊是要賠自摸最大番數」
- Worked example (Family A): 「例如「一二蚊」上限八番（即出銃八番玩家要付256元、其餘玩家要付128元）」 — note this incidental figure is the only **half-payment sum in real currency** anywhere in the corpus (256/128/128 = the 2:1:1 ratio).
- Family A on the dealer: 「如果食詐糊，該局結束，玩家重新洗牌開展下一局。但如食詐糊的玩家是莊家，也不可繼續連莊。」

## 8. WHAT IS A FACT, WHAT IS A CONVENTION — the decision list

**FACTS (multi-family agreement, no meaningful dissent):**
1. Core tile set 108 suited + 16 winds + 12 dragons; 4 flowers + 4 seasons on top = 144. No animals.
2. Self-draw pays 1.5× a discard win, split equally among three losers, under **both** payment conventions.
3. No dealer multiplier in HKOS (D explains its removal; B denies it; A and C's tables omit it).
4. Dealer repeats on a dealer win.
5. The 1-faan tier: 平糊, 自摸, each dragon triplet, seat wind, prevailing wind (2 when doubled), 搶槓, 海底撈月, 無花, 正花.
6. 對對糊 3, 混一色 3, 小三元 5, 清一色 7, 大三元 8, 字一色 10, 清幺九 10, 一台花 2, 花幺 4.
7. The fan-to-points curve is half-spicy: doubling every two faan from 4 upward. Only the base unit differs between sources.

**CONVENTIONS — you must choose, and no source can choose for you:**
1. **Minimum fan.** 3 is the overwhelming default; 0/1/2 are historically attested; B's own baseline says "0-2".
2. **Cap.** 8 / 10 / 13 / none. 10 is the official body's answer and the modal one.
3. **Payment convention.** 全銃 (modern, official, all published tables) vs 陪銃/出銃么二 (historically original per D and A).
4. **Washout → dealer keeps or passes.** Split down the middle, including inside individual sources.
5. **The six conflicted hands:** 小四喜 6 vs 10, 大四喜 13 vs 15, 十八羅漢 13 vs 18, 九子連環 10 vs 15 (10 better supported), 天糊/地糊 13 vs 10, 坎坎糊 8 vs 10.
6. **Whether 門前清 scores when flowers are in play** (A says it does not; C scores it; B omits it entirely).
7. **Whether 七對子 exists at all** in your ruleset (A: not standard 清章; B: 5, contested; C: silent).
8. **Whether 例牌 stack with ordinary fan.** A states non-stacking per hand (「例牌不另計其他牌型」) but reverses it for unlimited play (「打無限番…例牌亦都按普通番種計算番數」). McGill stacks limit hands with each other. **No default is stated by anyone.**
